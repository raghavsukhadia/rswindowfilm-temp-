Why Window Film?

The sun enters through your glass.
So does the heat.

Indian driving conditions mean intense sunlight, cabin heat, UV exposure and glare. RS Window Films are designed to reduce solar heat while maintaining a clean, comfortable view from inside your car.

Hot Cabin

Your parked car quickly absorbs solar heat through its glass.

High AC Load

More incoming heat means your air-conditioning has to work harder to maintain cabin comfort.

UV Exposure

Continuous UV exposure affects passengers as well as dashboards, upholstery and interior trim.

Driving Glare

Strong sunlight can make daytime driving more tiring and uncomfortable.

RS Window Film helps control what enters through your glass — while keeping the outside world clear.
