Global Series Heat Control Window Films

Affordable performance with carbon-infused technology. Designed for everyday comfort, durability, and reliable heat control.

What is Global Series?

Global Series window films are engineered with microscopic carbon particles infused directly into the polyester base material. This advanced construction enhances durability, improves heat rejection, and delivers reliable protection without affecting electronic signal clarity.
