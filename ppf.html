Where Can PPF Be Applied?

Common areas include the front bumper, bonnet, fenders, ORVMs, door edges, headlights, side skirts and other high-impact areas. For complete protection, PPF can also be applied across the entire vehicle.

Paint Protection Film

Invisible Protection. Original Finish.

Protect your vehicle’s original paint from the everyday damage of the road.

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a transparent, durable film applied to your vehicle’s painted surfaces to help defend against stone chips, scratches, road debris, stains and everyday wear—while keeping the original finish visible.

01 — STONE CHIP PROTECTION

Helps protect vulnerable paint from road debris and flying stones.

02 — SCRATCH RESISTANCE

Adds an extra layer of protection against everyday surface scratches.

03 — PAINT PRESERVATION

Helps maintain the original paint and finish of your vehicle.

04 — INVISIBLE FINISH

Clear protection designed to preserve the original look of your car.

A Simple Way to Protect Your Paint

PPF doesn't change the look of your car. It simply adds a protective layer over the original paint, helping you keep the finish looking good through everyday driving.
