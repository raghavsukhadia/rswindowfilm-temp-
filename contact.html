Frequently Asked Questions

What are the benefits of RS Window Film?

RS Window Film helps reduce heat, glare and harmful UV rays while improving cabin comfort and helping protect your car’s interior from fading.

Does RS Window Film block harmful UV rays?

Yes. Selected RS Window Film products provide up to 99% UV protection, helping protect passengers and vehicle interiors from prolonged UV exposure.

Will window film make my car too dark?

No. RS offers different VLT options, including high-visibility films designed to maintain a clear driving view while providing heat and UV protection.

Does window film interfere with GPS or mobile signals?

Selected RS Window Films are designed to be signal-friendly, helping maintain normal GPS, mobile, Bluetooth and electronic connectivity.

How long does window film installation take?

Installation time depends on the vehicle and selected coverage, but most standard installations can generally be completed within a few hours.

What is Paint Protection Film (PPF)?

PPF is a transparent protective layer applied over your vehicle’s paint to help protect against minor scratches, stone chips, stains and everyday road damage.

Where can PPF be applied on a car?

PPF can be applied to areas such as the bonnet, bumper, fenders, ORVMs, door edges, headlights and side skirts, or across the entire vehicle for more complete protection.

Does RS Window Film and PPF come with a warranty?

Selected RS Window Film and PPF products are covered by warranty according to the specific product and installation terms. Warranty duration and coverage may vary by product.
