Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

RS Window Film respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we may collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit www.rswindowfilm.com, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

Information you provide directly

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Vehicle details
  • Product or service requirements
  • Enquiry or quotation information
  • Messages or communications you send us

Your current quote form collects a name, phone number, optional email address, and service-selection information.

Information collected automatically

When you use the website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Approximate location
  • Pages visited
  • Referral source
  • Time spent on the website
  • Website interaction information

This may occur through cookies, analytics services, or similar technologies if enabled on the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Provide quotations
  • Recommend suitable products or services
  • Schedule installations
  • Provide customer support
  • Handle warranty enquiries
  • Contact you regarding your request
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent misuse or fraud
  • Maintain business records
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

Personal information should only be used for legitimate purposes connected with your interaction with RS Window Film.

3. Consent

When you voluntarily submit personal information through our website or enquiry forms, we may process that information for the purpose communicated to you.

Where consent is required, users should be given clear information about what data is collected and why it is being collected. India’s DPDP framework also provides for withdrawal of consent and exercise of data-related rights.

You may contact us if you wish to withdraw consent for optional communications.

4. Marketing Communications

We may contact you regarding products or services you have enquired about.

Promotional communications should only be sent where permitted by applicable law or where you have provided appropriate consent.

You may request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality
  • Remember preferences
  • Understand website usage
  • Measure website performance
  • Improve the user experience

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling some cookies may affect certain website functionality.

6. Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how visitors use our website.

Analytics services may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Device information
  • Browser information
  • Referral sources
  • Interaction data

This information helps us improve website usability and performance.

7. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information only where reasonably necessary with:

  • Authorized employees
  • Installation or service teams
  • Technology providers
  • Website hosting providers
  • Communication providers
  • Professional advisers
  • Government or regulatory authorities where legally required

Service providers should only receive information necessary for performing their services.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect personal information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Loss
  • Misuse
  • Alteration
  • Disclosure
  • Destruction

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

Personal information will generally be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable law.

For example, enquiry details may be retained for customer service, warranty, business-record, or follow-up purposes where appropriate.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request updating of information
  • Request deletion or erasure where applicable
  • Withdraw previously provided consent
  • Raise a complaint or concern regarding your personal information

The DPDP Act provides individuals with rights relating to access to information about personal-data processing, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and consent management, subject to applicable conditions.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and automotive services are not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children through our quotation forms without appropriate authorization.

If you believe information relating to a child has been submitted improperly, please contact us.

12. External Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or social-media platforms.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external platforms.

Users should review the privacy policies of those services separately.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in:

  • Our services
  • Website features
  • Technology
  • Business practices
  • Applicable laws

The latest version will be published on this page.
