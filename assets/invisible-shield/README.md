# RS Invisible Shield assets

Scene generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Served as responsive AVIF/WebP; the 48px WebP is the progressive fallback. Both comparison states use one image.

## Generation prompt

Create a photorealistic architectural photograph for a premium mobile window-film experience. Portrait composition approximately 2:3, no text, no logos, no graphics, no people. Camera inside a sophisticated charcoal minimalist room, looking straight at one very tall large clear rectangular architectural window. Slim black metal frame, window spanning approximately x=12%-88% and y=10%-80%, precise straight vertical edges, NOT a grid of multiple panes. Outside is crisp blue sky, elegant distant pale concrete architecture and leafy green trees, realistic natural depth. Daylight entering from the upper right, restrained soft sunlight across a stone interior sill and floor. Dark charcoal interior walls around window. Lower 20% mostly dark charcoal foreground floor providing negative space for an HTML brand lockup; upper edge dark charcoal. Photographic, restrained premium architectural editorial, natural neutral white balance, plausible materials and reflections, refined subtle glass reflections. Do not bake orange warmth, visible particle rays, red edges, glare bloom, split comparisons or text into image: these will be animated separately. Exterior must remain clearly visible and natural, not tinted black or blue. Compose the window as central hero with safe margins for portrait phone crops. Deliver one image.
